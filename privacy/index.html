Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

The short version

Farfound doesn't collect any data about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps your progress on your device: your points, streak and best stop, the rockets you've unlocked, your settings, the card types you've switched on, the player names you type for Space Race and Orbit, and your flight plan callsign. We never receive any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Flight plan links

When you choose to send a flight plan, the app builds a link that holds your callsign, the prompts you flew and, for each one, your answer, its score and how quickly you answered. You choose who gets it and which app sends it, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Anyone who has the link can see what's in it.

Opening a link goes to the page at v8l8z.github.io/farfound/plan. Everything after the # in the link is read inside your browser and is never sent to the web server.

No third-party services in the app

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. Its fonts and all its prompts are built in, and it doesn't contact any server while you play. It only goes online when you tap a link that opens a web page.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, covered by Google's privacy policy. The website sets no cookies.

Children

Farfound is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date at the top.

Contact

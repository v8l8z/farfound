FARFOUND

Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

The short version

Farfound has no account and no analytics of its own. Everything the game remembers stays on your phone, or in your browser when you play on the web. There are two exceptions. An online match with a friend passes through our match server while you play it, and is deleted within a few hours. And from version 1.3, the iPhone app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data from your device to do so; the website has no ads.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps your progress on your device: your points, streak and best stop, the rockets you've unlocked, your settings, the card types you've switched on, the player names you type for Space Race and Orbit, and your flight plan callsign. We never receive any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Flight plan links

When you choose to send a flight plan, the app builds a link that holds your callsign, the prompts you flew and, for each one, your answer, its score and how quickly you answered. You choose who gets it and which app sends it, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Anyone who has the link can see what's in it.

Opening a link goes to the page at farfoundtrivia.com/plan, or straight into the app if you have it. Everything after the # in the link is read inside your browser and is never sent to the web server.

Online matches

An online match is the only part of Farfound that uses a server. When you create or join one, the game sends our match server the match code, the nickname you typed (you can leave it blank), which rocket you fly, and for each prompt your answer, its score and how quickly you answered. The server passes these to the other player in your match so each of you can see how the other is doing, and to nobody else.

A match isn't linked to an account, a device identifier or anything else that says who you are, and we don't use it for advertising or analytics. It is deleted from the server two hours after its last activity. The match server runs on Cloudflare, which handles the connection and may process your IP address to deliver it, as described in the Cloudflare privacy policy. If you never play an online match, the game never contacts the match server.

Ads in the iPhone app

From version 1.3, the iPhone app shows a full-screen ad after some games. The ads come from Google AdMob, and they are the only third-party code in the app. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google collects data from your device: your IP address, which gives a rough location, your device's advertising identifier if you allow it, and details of how you interact with ads and how the ad code performs. Google and its advertising partners may use this to show you ads based on your interests. We never receive this data ourselves, and Google is not sent your answers, scores or anything you type. How Google uses data from apps that use its services explains more.

You have choices. The first time the app opens, iPhone asks whether to allow tracking; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google doesn't get your advertising identifier and ads are less personal. You can change this at any time in the Settings app, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Where the law requires your consent for ads, as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for it before showing any, and a "Privacy choices" button under How to play lets you change your answer. We ask Google to show only ads it rates as suitable for general audiences.

The app contains no analytics code. Its fonts and all its prompts are built in, and apart from ads and online matches it doesn't contact any server while you play. Versions before 1.3 have no ads at all.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, covered by Google's privacy policy. The website sets no cookies.

You can also play Farfound on this website, at farfoundtrivia.com/play. It keeps your progress in your browser's storage on your own device, the same things the app keeps on your phone, and never sends it anywhere. Its fonts come with the game, so that page loads nothing from Google. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your progress.

Children

Farfound is made for a general audience, not specifically for children. It has no accounts and asks for no personal details from anyone. A nickname in an online match is optional and is deleted with the match. Ads in the iPhone app are limited to those Google rates as suitable for general audiences.

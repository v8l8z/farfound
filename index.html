Farfound

A daily word game where rare answers fly further.

Every day, everyone in the world gets the same eight prompts, like Name a city further north than London. You have 20 seconds for each. Answers few other players would think of burn more thrust, and your rocket flies further along 31 real stops from a launch pad on Earth to the Andromeda galaxy.

Support

Help with Farfound, and how to reach us.

Get in touch

Found a bug, or an answer that should have counted? Open a support request and tell us your iPhone model and iOS version. You can also read earlier requests.

How to play

Each flight has eight prompts. Type an answer in 20 seconds. The rarer your answer, the more thrust it burns and the further your rocket flies along 31 stops from Earth to the Andromeda galaxy. Tap the question mark in the game for the full rules.

Common questions

My answer was right but didn't count.
In the app, an answer counts when it's in that prompt's logbook, which holds hundreds of answers per prompt. If a correct one is missing, send it to us and we'll add it in an update.
Does everyone get the same Today's flight?
Yes. The same eight prompts everywhere in the world, new every day at midnight UTC.
How do I play with people in the same room?
Open Space Race or Orbit under Play together and pass the phone round. Both work for 2 to 6 players, with a relaxed 30-second clock if you want one.
How do flight plans work?
After a flight, tap Send flight plan and share the link. Your friend opens it, taps Open in Farfound and flies the same prompts with your ghost beside them. Then they can send it back to you, or on to someone else.
I lost my progress.
Progress is stored only on your phone, so deleting the app removes it. There's no account to restore it from.
What do you do with my data?
Nothing, because the app doesn't collect any. See the privacy policy.
